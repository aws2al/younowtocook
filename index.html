About sopbyme

At sopbyme, we champion fashion without boundaries. Our collections are designed specifically for unisex wear, prioritizing fluid silhouettes, inclusive tailoring, and superior fabrics.

We believe clothing should adapt to your personal expression rather than conventional labels. Every piece is paired with practical dressing video guides to help you style your look with confidence.

1. Personal Data Collection

When you purchase apparel or interact with sopbyme, we collect your name, shipping address, email address (for order tracking), and payment verification details.

Collected details are strictly used to fulfill clothing shipments, provide customer support, process secure payments, and send order updates.

3. Payment & Data Security

sopbyme does not store credit card or banking details directly on our servers. All transactions are securely processed through encrypted third-party gateways.
